**Who we are**

### Entrepreneurs Meet is where good ideas and good people find each other.

Over 3 years, we have been building a not-for-profit community of entrepreneurs in Cagliari, run by its members. It started with one question: why are so many people here building alone?

## 03 · Pitch · What we do

footer: What we do

**What we do**

### This isn’t your average networking event.

- Monthly The meetup Last Thursday of every month, 19:00, a different venue each month. Kickoff with collaborative challenges, then real conversations and open networking. Thirty-five events and counting.
- Twice a year The headline events Kickoff dinner in January. The Summer Party in August at Torre delle Stelle.
- Between The formats that grew around it NetWalking on the Bastione. Hustle & Grow workshops. Working Days. Brunch with an investor.
- Always The platform entrepreneursmeet.it — events listing & ticketing, a member directory, a library of events and knowledge base, a blog. Built by members, for members.

## 04 · Pitch · Why it matters

footer: Why it matters

**Our vision & our goal**

Put Cagliari on the world map — and make sure everyone who arrives finds belonging.

Talent, values-driven people and capital, drawn to the island and the good life. We see a Cagliari that stands as a Mediterranean capital of ventures: inclusive, cultured, globally minded.

## 07 · Session A

footer: Session A of four

**Session A · Talks**

### Founder stories, told in short form.

Our monthly format, lifted onto the conference stage: four or five members, seven minutes each, one real thing they learned.

Format

Lightning talks, moderated.

Length

45 minutes.

Who it’s for

Anyone early on the road, wanting proof it can be done here.

## 08 · Session B

footer: Session B of four

**Session B · Workshop**

### Round tables on one hard question.

Facilitated tables of eight. Each takes one problem, works it for forty minutes and reports back. Audio is captured and outcomes are delivered.

Format

Facilitated round tables.

Length

60 minutes.

Who it’s for

Operators and stakeholders that care about a cause or a mission.

Problem / topic to be decided with the Innovate Her team.

## 09 · Session C

footer: Session C of four

**Session C · Networking**

### The aperitivo, facilitated by us.

The part of our evenings people come back for, at the end of the main Innovate Her event. Hosts at every table so nobody stands alone with a drink.

Format

Hosted networking.

Length

90 minutes, close of day.

Who it’s for

Everyone. Especially the ones who came alone.

## 10 · Session D

footer: Session D of four

**Session D · NetWalking + Aperitivo**

Format

Guided tour and aperitivo.

Length

45 minute tour, 2 hr aperitivo.

Who it’s for

Everyone. Especially the ones who came alone to Cagliari.
